Reliable Individual-Tree Inventory From Airborne Laser Scanning With Competition-Aware Segmentation and Trait-Specific Selective Delivery

Two training-only objectives are added to a query-based 3-D segmenter: ACPE separates cross-fitted tree-level prototypes in shallow voxel features, and EPCO supervises point ownership where crowns touch. Predicted and reference trees are then measured with one frozen definition of height and crown width, every reference tree stays in the denominator, and a reliability-control stage ranks what a human should check. Inference is unchanged: both objectives are removed after training.

Selective delivery

  • Every reference tree stays in the denominator, so omitted and undeliverable trees are counted rather than dropped.
  • Three risk heads score instance validity, height failure and crown-width failure from prediction-only descriptors, validated by leave-one-physical-source-out.
  • At a nominal 20% review budget the queue intercepts 72% of invalid instances, 70% of height failures and 42% of crown-width failures, 3.5, 3.3 and 2.0 times the yield of random review.
  • On an external field-measured source that took no part in development, all four configurations deliver both traits for the 43 reference trees within tolerance, and the frozen validity ranking transfers with an AUROC of 0.86.
Manuscript submitted to IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing. Method and evaluation code: github.com/Harry33t/als-tree-inventory-trc. Evaluation uses the openly available FOR-instanceV2 benchmark; the external field-measured source is available from the corresponding author on request.
